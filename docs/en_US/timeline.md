# Timeline
**Analysis → Timeline**

## Timeline

The Timeline page displays events in chronological order, such as changes to *info* commands, triggers of *action* commands, and scenario executions.

To view them, you must first enable tracking on the timeline for the desired commands or scenarios, and then wait for those events to occur.

- **Scenario**: Either directly on a scenario page or on the *Overview* of scenarios.
- **Command**: Either in the command’s advanced settings or in the history settings to perform a “bulk” action.

![Timeline](../images/timeline_intro.jpg)

The *Main* timeline always contains all events. However, you can filter the timeline by *folder*. Whenever you open the timeline, you’ll see a field where you can enter the name of a folder—whether it exists or not.
You can then filter the timeline by this folder by selecting it to the left of the *Refresh* button.

> **Note**
>
> If you no longer use a folder, it will remain in the list as long as there are events associated with that folder. It will then disappear from the list on its own.

Once you've enabled tracking for the desired commands and scenarios in the timeline, you'll see them appear on the timeline.

> **Important**
>
> After enabling tracking on the timeline, you must wait for new events to appear before you can see them.

## Display

The timeline displays recorded events, arranged vertically by day.

For each event, you have:

- The date and time of the event,
- Event type: An info or action command, or a scenario, with the command plugin used for the commands.
- The parent object's name, the name, and—depending on the type—the status or trigger.

- A command-type event displays an icon on the right to open the command's settings.
- A scenario-type event displays two icons on the right to access the scenario or open the scenario log.

In the upper-right corner, you can select a timeline folder. This folder must be created beforehand and must contain events.
