# Health
**Analysis → Health**

The Jeedom Health page gives you a quick overview of your Jeedom’s health status.
It also displays (if the plugins use it) the health status for each plugin.

> **Note**
>
> Click the icon in the upper-right corner to access your Jeedom's benchmark.

The Jeedom health page is displayed as a dual table.
The first column describes what Jeedom checks, and the status is displayed in the second column.
Tooltips can provide troubleshooting tips if the check fails.

Below, you'll find the status of each plugin; clicking on a plugin's name will display its status details.

On the right, you'll find the following icons:

-   Access port for the plugin's hardware (type: Z-Wave key).
-   Daemon mode.
-   Displaying the plugin's device health page.
-   Displaying the plugin's configuration page.
-   Overall status of the plugin.

> **Note**
>
> Icons may vary depending on the plugin.

> **Note**
>
> Generally speaking, when the line is green, everything is fine, and when it's red, there's a problem.
